FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions about CampusFind.

Click on "Report Lost Item" in the navigation menu or on the homepage. Fill in the form with as much detail as possible — including item name, category, location, date lost, and a description. Submit the form and your report will be visible to all users immediately.
Great job being a good Samaritan! Go to "Report Found Item", fill in the details about where and when you found it, upload a photo if possible, and submit. The item will appear in the Found Items listing so the owner can contact you. You can also hand it in to your campus security office or administration building.
Yes, CampusFind is completely free for all university students, teaching staff, and administrative staff. There are no fees, subscriptions, or premium plans. The platform is a university service.
Open the item listing and click "View Details". On the detail page, click "Contact Reporter" to see the contact information of the person who submitted the found item report. Please be respectful and verify your ownership of the item before claiming it.
Once you've recovered your item, please log into your dashboard and delete or mark your report as resolved. This helps keep the listings clean and lets others know the item has been returned. Thank you for keeping CampusFind up to date!
Try broadening your search terms or clearing your filters. Search by a single keyword like "wallet" or "phone" rather than a long phrase. Also check both the Lost Items and Found Items sections. If you still can't find it, submit a lost item report so others can notify you if they find it.
You can browse lost and found items without an account. However, to submit a report, contact a reporter, or use the dashboard, you need to create a free account with your university email and student ID.
CampusFind is a frontend prototype that currently stores data locally in your browser. Your contact information is only visible to people who view the item details page. In the full production version, all data will be encrypted, stored securely, and never sold or shared with third parties.
