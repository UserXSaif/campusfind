Simple Process

How CampusFind Works

Four simple steps to recover your lost belongings.

1
📝

Report an Item

Submit a detailed report of your lost item or an item you found on campus. Include photos and location details.

2
🔍

Search Listings

Browse through found items or search by category, location, or description to find your belonging.

3
📞

Contact the User

Once you find a match, contact the person who reported the item directly through the platform.

4
🎉

Recover Your Item

Arrange a meeting at a safe campus location, verify ownership, and recover your belonging.
