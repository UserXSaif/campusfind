About CampusFind

Reuniting Campus
Communities

A centralized platform that makes it easy for university students and staff to report, search, and recover lost belongings — all in one place.

Our Mission

Why CampusFind Exists

Every semester, hundreds of students and staff lose valuable personal items on university campuses. Phones, wallets, ID cards, keys, laptops — the list goes on. Without a centralized system, most lost items are never recovered.

CampusFind was created to solve this problem. By providing a single, accessible platform where anyone can report or browse lost and found items, we dramatically increase the chances that belongings are reunited with their owners.

Whether you've lost something or found something, CampusFind makes the process simple, transparent, and fast.

Key Benefits

What Makes CampusFind Useful

Designed specifically for the university community with features that matter.

🗂️

Centralized Database

All lost and found reports are stored in one place. No more posting on multiple notice boards or Facebook groups — one search covers everything.

🔍

Smart Search & Filter

Search by item name, category, location, or date. Filter results to quickly narrow down to exactly what you're looking for.

📱

Mobile Friendly

Fully responsive on phones, tablets, and desktops. Report an item or check for updates from anywhere on campus.

🔒

Secure Contact System

Contact details are shared only when needed. The platform encourages safe, on-campus item exchanges in public areas.

⚡

Fast Reporting

Submit a lost or found item report in under 2 minutes. Quick form, clear categories, and helpful prompts guide you through the process.

📊

Personal Dashboard

Track all your reports in one place. See the status of your lost items, manage your found item posts, and view resolved cases.

For Everyone on Campus

Who Can Use CampusFind?

🎓

Students

Undergraduate and postgraduate students who frequently move around campus and are most likely to lose or find items. CampusFind is built primarily with you in mind.

👩‍🏫

Teaching Staff

Professors, lecturers, and teaching assistants who work across multiple buildings and classrooms. Quick access for reporting or checking for lost belongings.

🏢

University Staff

Administrative staff, security officers, librarians, and other support staff who often find items left behind and need a reliable place to report them.

The Process

How It Works in Detail

1

Create an Account

Register with your university email and student ID. Your account lets you manage all your reports in one place.

2

Submit Your Report

Fill in the item details — name, category, location, date, and a description. Add a photo if possible to make identification easier.

3

Search & Match

Browse found items or search by keyword. The system shows you all relevant reports that could match your lost item.

✓

Recover Your Belonging

Contact the finder, arrange a safe on-campus handover, and mark your report as resolved.

Roadmap

Future Development Plans

CampusFind is currently a frontend prototype. Here's what's planned for future phases.

🗄️

Backend & Database

A full backend API with a relational database to permanently store user accounts, item reports, and status tracking — replacing the current localStorage prototype.

🔔

Email Notifications

Automatic email alerts when a newly reported found item matches your lost item description. Real-time push notifications via the mobile browser.

💬

In-App Messaging

Direct, private messaging between reporters and finders — eliminating the need to share personal email addresses publicly on the platform.
